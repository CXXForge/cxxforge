# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CXXForge is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Discuss technical ideas critically without attacking people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to evidence and correction.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, community channels, events, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in CXXForge.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers through the contact route published in the GitHub organization profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Maintainers will respect confidentiality to the extent possible and may take proportionate action, including warnings, content moderation, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code. Enforcement decisions should be documented appropriately, handled consistently, and made with regard to the safety and well-being of participants.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant. The project may adopt a specific upstream version as its community grows.
